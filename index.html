Checkers Sixty60 3D Model & Rigging Blueprint

1. Van Rigging & Bone Hierarchy

To ensure perfect arcade suspension, wheel spin, and door kinematics, export your Blender van model with the following hierarchy or node names:

CheckersHyperVan (Root Object / Armature)
├── chassis (Base body mesh, responds to pitch/roll)
│ ├── door_driver (Hinged pivot at front-left edge: rot Z/Y 0° to 65°)
│ ├── door_slide (Sliding cargo door: translate X +0.08m, translate Z -1.05m)
│ ├── door_rear_L (Left barn door: hinged at outer pillar, rot Y -120°)
│ ├── door_rear_R (Right barn door: hinged at outer pillar, rot Y +120°)
│ ├── steering_wheel (Hinged at column: rot Z with steering)
│ └── cargo_bed (Container holding paper delivery bags)
├── wheel_FL (Front-left wheel pivot: yaw for steer, pitch for spin)
├── wheel_FR (Front-right wheel pivot: yaw for steer, pitch for spin)
├── wheel_RL (Rear-left wheel pivot: pitch for spin)
└── wheel_RR (Rear-right wheel pivot: pitch for spin)

2. Mixamo Rigged Driver Character

Your character uses the standard Mixamo Armature structure. When exporting from Blender, ensure:

  • Real World Scale: Character height ~1.80m (Blender Unit Scale: 1.0, Metric, Meters).
  • Standard Mixamo Bone Names: mixamorigHips, mixamorigSpine, mixamorigHead, mixamorigLeftHand, etc.
  • Animation Clips to Include: Idle, Walk, Run, CarryBag, OpenDoor, SitDrive.
